Project information

Read the date alongside the number.

This directory contains 93 selected condo and EC projects. Every project page links to its ERA source. Some projects are selling, some are at preview and others remain preliminary launches.

Availability changes; counts with conflicting portal totals are withheld for confirmation. An unpublished count does not establish that a type is sold out.

Portal TOP dates are estimates. The developer documents and sale contract govern completion obligations. School and station distances are location estimates; use MOE and OneMap to check school registration requirements.

How the browse areas work

Central, East, West, North and North East are broad editorial groups based on each project’s location. The neighbourhood labels sometimes combine adjacent places. They help you explore the directory and are not official URA planning boundaries or CCR/RCR/OCR market regions.

School cards show a selection of primary schools represented in the portal data. They are not a ranking. MRT categories include planned stations, which are identified on project pages. Planned status is separate from the portal’s completion-year field, which can be missing or outdated. Future opening years are not reproduced from that field. Check LTA’s rail-development update for the official programme. All counts refer only to this directory’s dated inventory.
