2027 launch guide

New launch condos in Singapore, 2027

What is launching in 2027, where it is and how firm the timing is. Dates here come from developers and public sources. Where a date is not confirmed, I say so.

What is coming in 2027

ERA’s project marketing team counts 16 launches and about 7,455 homes for the first half of 2027: 12 private condos and 4 executive condos.

I have grouped them by how firm the timing is. The first group has a launch window from the developer itself. The second is on ERA’s first-half list but has no date from its developer. The third has no launch date at all.

There are no prices on this page, because most of these projects have not released any. For what the homes around a launch have sold for, read my River Opus pricing preview and the other pricing articles and project analysis.
