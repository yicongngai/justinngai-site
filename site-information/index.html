Property information

This is Justin Ngai’s personal real-estate website. Project facts and availability are dated snapshots, with sources linked on the relevant pages. Confirm the current unit, availability, specifications and terms before making a decision. Developer documents and the sale contract govern the purchase.

Written guides and recorded commentary explain how to compare property choices. Examples are illustrative, and opinions are not promises of future prices, rental income or investment returns. Some videos contain historical prices or transaction comparisons that may no longer apply.

Developer renderings are artist’s impressions. Selected home tours show previously recorded homes and do not establish current availability. School proximity does not guarantee admission; school logos identify the schools and do not imply endorsement.

Enquiries and privacy

This website has no enquiry form, advertising pixel or site analytics script. Contact buttons open WhatsApp or your telephone app. You choose whether to send a message or place a call; visiting a page does not send an enquiry.

If you contact Justin, the information you choose to provide can be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the property services you request. Share only what is needed for that conversation. Avoid sending identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

The hosting service and external services such as WhatsApp, Instagram and linked websites handle information under their own policies. For questions about information shared with Justin, or a request to correct it or stop follow-up, call +65 9661 5511 or use the contact page.
