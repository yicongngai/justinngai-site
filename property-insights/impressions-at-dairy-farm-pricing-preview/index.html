Impressions at Dairy Farm pricing preview: Narra Residences next door sold its 3-bedrooms at $1.93M. Where will Impressions land?

No prices are out for Impressions at Dairy Farm yet. So I looked at what buyers paid for the homes around Dairy Farm and Hillview, bedroom for bedroom, and what my land price tool says it should launch at.

By Justin Ngai · Pricing preview · 26 November 2026

Impressions at Dairy Farm is the condo planned on the newest Dairy Farm Walk land sale site in District 23, by a group led by Roxy-Pacific Holdings. It is the third government site sold on Dairy Farm Walk since 2022. The Botany at Dairy Farm is about 140 m away and Narra Residences about 340 m.

That makes this one of the easier launches to read before the price list is out, because its two neighbours on the same road have already sold, on land of a similar price.

The short version

  • Narra Residences, the newest launch next door, sold its 3-bedrooms at a median of $1.93M and its 2-bedrooms at $1.44M.
  • Across the five nearest launches, a new 3-bedroom sold at $1.93M to $2.24M.
  • A resale 3-bedroom nearby went for $1.72M to $1.90M over the past 12 months, less than any new one.
  • The developers paid $962 psf ppr for the land. My land price tool puts the launch at about $2,354 psf on average.

Impressions at Dairy Farm price list, showflat and unit mix

The developer has not released the Impressions at Dairy Farm price list yet. The unit mix, floor plans, site plan and showflat date are not out either. What is firm today is the developer (the Roxy-Pacific led group), the land price and the plan for about 480 to 500 homes.

When the price list comes out, I will put each bedroom type against the neighbours below. If you want it the day it is released, message me.

The location

Hillview MRT on the Downtown Line is about 770 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit, and Cashew MRT about 910 m. Plan on a walk of ten minutes or more, or a bus. Dairy Farm Mall is about 400 m away, and Dairy Farm Nature Park is to the south.

The plot ratio of 1.4 means a low-rise project on a large, sloping site. Buyers here are usually choosing green surroundings over being right on top of an MRT station.

Straight-line distances from the middle of the site, measured on OneMap. Walking routes are longer.

Impressions at Dairy Farm land price

With a government land sale, I start from what the developer paid for the land, because that sets the floor for the launch price.

URA awarded the site in January 2026. It drew 5 bids and went for $427.0 million, or $962 per square foot of floor area (psf ppr).

The Botany, Hillhaven and Narra Residences all sat on land at $980 to $1,024, and they launched at $2,065 to $2,143 psf. Narra Residences, on $1,020 land, launched at $2,143 in January 2026.

Do not read the lower land price as a cheaper launch. The Sen’s land cost only $841, the lowest in the table, and it launched the highest, at $2,338 psf in November 2025. Land sets the floor. The launch price follows the market on the day.

Impressions at Dairy Farm expected launch price

My own estimate, from my land price tool, is that Impressions at Dairy Farm launches at an average of about $2,354 psf. The developer has not released prices, so I will update this once the price list is out.

For comparison:

  • $2,164 psf is the median at Narra Residences next door, launched Jan 2026.
  • $2,344 psf is the median at The Sen, the highest of the five launches.
  • $962 is what the developers paid for the land, close to what the two earlier Dairy Farm Walk sites cost.

The sizes are not out, so I cannot turn the psf into a price for a 3-bedroom yet. When they are, I would put the 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom prices against Narra Residences and The Botany, bedroom for bedroom, before looking at anything else. If you are deciding between waiting for Impressions and buying at Narra Residences or a resale home nearby, message me and I will run the comparison for the home you are looking at.

How I worked this out

  • Prices are from URA caveats, up to November 2026. November is not a full month yet.
  • For the new launches I used every new sale since launch. For resale I used the past 12 months.
  • I left out executive condominiums, because they are sold under different rules.
  • URA does not record bedrooms, so I matched each sale’s floor area to the project’s unit types from its floor plans and listings. A sale I could not match is left out of the bedroom columns.
  • A box says “Too few sales” where fewer than three sales matched.
  • The neighbours’ figures are past sales. The launch estimate is my own working figure, from my land price tool, and is not the developer’s price.
