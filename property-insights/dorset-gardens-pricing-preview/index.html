Dorset Gardens pricing preview: Piccadilly Grand, 450 m away, now resells at $2,456 psf. Where will Dorset Gardens land?

No prices are out for Dorset Gardens yet. So I looked at what buyers paid for the homes around Farrer Park, bedroom for bedroom, and what my land price tool says it should launch at.

By Justin Ngai · Pricing preview · 26 November 2026

Dorset Gardens is the condo planned on the Dorset Road land sale site in Farrer Park, District 8. About 425 homes, by UOL Group, Singapore Land Group and Kheng Leong, a short walk from Farrer Park MRT.

The obvious comparison is Piccadilly Grand, about 450 m away. It was the last big launch in this neighbourhood, it sits on a government land sale site too, and its owners have started reselling. So it shows both what buyers paid at launch and what the market pays now.

The short version

  • Piccadilly Grand buyers paid a median of $2,142 psf at launch. Over the past 12 months its homes resold at $2,456 psf, about 15% more.
  • A resale 3-bedroom there now goes for $2.67M, against $2.26M at launch.
  • The developers paid $1,338 psf ppr for the Dorset Road land, about 19% more than Piccadilly Grand’s land. Every site sold nearby since has gone higher.
  • My land price tool puts the Dorset Gardens launch at about $2,806 psf on average.

Dorset Gardens price list, showflat and unit mix

The developer has not released the Dorset Gardens price list yet. The unit mix, floor plans, site plan and showflat date are not out either. What is firm today is the developer (UOL Group, Singapore Land Group and Kheng Leong), the land price and the plan for about 425 homes.

When the price list comes out, I will put each bedroom type against the neighbours below. If you want it the day it is released, message me.

The location

The site is on Dorset Road, a few streets north of Farrer Park MRT on the North East Line, about 220 m from the middle of the site to the nearest exit. Little India MRT, for the Downtown Line, is about 860 m away.

Farrer Park is one stop from Little India and two from Dhoby Ghaut on the North East Line. Most of the other new projects around here are small freehold blocks, which is why Piccadilly Grand is the closest comparison.

Straight-line distances from the middle of the site, measured on OneMap. Walking routes are longer.

Dorset Gardens land price

With a government land sale, I start from what the developer paid for the land, because that sets the floor for the launch price.

URA awarded the Dorset Road site in October 2025. It drew 9 bids and went for $524.3 million, or $1,338 per square foot of floor area (psf ppr).

Piccadilly Grand’s land cost $1,129 and it launched at $2,178 psf in May 2022. Dorset Road cost about 19% more. The Orie in Toa Payoh is the nearest match on land, at $1,360, and it launched at $2,730 psf in January 2025.

Land has not become cheaper since. The three sites sold nearby after Dorset Road all went higher:

  • Kallang Close, about 1.6 km away: $1,415 in April 2026

Piccadilly Grand is the one to watch. Its 3-bedrooms resold at a median of $2.67M over the past 12 months, the highest in the table, and its 2-bedrooms at $1.75M. Its owners bought at a median of $2,142 psf and are now reselling at $2,456.

The older 99-year condos around Farrer Park resold their 3-bedrooms at $1.52M to $2.62M. They are cheaper, but they also have fewer years left on the lease and older facilities.

Resale prices here have moved up for years. All three condos in the chart below rose every year from 2022 to 2026. At Kerrisdale the median went from $1,313 psf to $1,661 psf, about 27%.

Dorset Gardens expected launch price

My own estimate, from my land price tool, is that Dorset Gardens launches at an average of about $2,806 psf. The developer has not released prices, so I will update this once the price list is out.

For comparison:

  • $2,456 psf is what Piccadilly Grand resells at today, for a home that is already built.
  • $2,730 psf is what The Orie launched at, on land that cost about the same as Dorset Road.
  • $1,338 is what the developers paid for the land. The three sites sold nearby since then went for more.

The sizes are not out, so I cannot turn the psf into a price for a 3-bedroom yet. When they are, I would put the 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom prices against Piccadilly Grand’s resale prices, bedroom for bedroom, before looking at anything else. If you are deciding between waiting for Dorset Gardens and buying a resale home around Farrer Park, message me and I will run the comparison for the home you are looking at.

How I worked this out

  • Prices are from URA caveats, up to November 2026. November is not a full month yet.
  • For the new launches I used every new sale since launch. For resale I used the past 12 months.
  • I left out executive condominiums, because they are sold under different rules.
  • URA does not record bedrooms, so I matched each sale’s floor area to the project’s unit types from its floor plans and listings. A sale I could not match is left out of the bedroom columns.
  • A box says “Too few sales” where fewer than three sales matched.
  • The neighbours’ figures are past sales. The launch estimate is my own working figure, from my land price tool, and is not the developer’s price.
