One Chuan Grove pricing preview: Chuan Park sold its 3-bedrooms at $2.61M. What comes next?

No prices are out for One Chuan Grove yet. So I looked at what buyers paid for the homes around Lorong Chuan, bedroom for bedroom.

By Justin Ngai · Pricing preview · 14 November 2026

One Chuan Grove is the next launch at Lorong Chuan, by Sing Holdings and Sunway Developments. It will have 1,056 homes on two government land sites joined into one. Sing Holdings said in August 2026 that the sales launch is on track for the first quarter of 2027.

The last launch at Lorong Chuan was Chuan Park, in November 2024. The Orie in Toa Payoh followed in January 2025 and Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill in October 2026. Between the three, about 2,800 new homes have been sold.

There is no price list yet. So for now I compare one thing: what the same number of bedrooms costs in the homes around it, new and resale.

The short version

  • A new 2-bedroom at the three launches sold at a median of $1.85M to $1.95M, and a new 3-bedroom at $2.61M to $2.94M.
  • A resale 3-bedroom in the 99-year condos around Lorong Chuan sold at $2.13M to $2.69M over the past 12 months.
  • The developers paid $1,376 and $1,331 per square foot of floor area for the two sites. The Orie’s land cost $1,360, and it launched at $2,730 psf.
  • The newest land sale in the area, at Sin Ming Avenue, went for $1,612 in September 2026.

Unit mix

So far the developer has only given the size range, 474 to 1,561 sq ft, and that can still change. How many 2-bedroom, 3-bedroom and larger homes there will be is not out yet, and neither are the floor plans or the site plan.

So I cannot tell you yet how big a 3-bedroom here will be. Because the price you pay is the size times the psf, nobody can work out the final price until the sizes are out. I will update this page when the unit mix is released.

The location

The site is at Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan. Lorong Chuan MRT on the Circle Line is the nearest station, about 250 m away on Sing Holdings’ figure. From there it is one stop to Bishan for the North South Line and one stop to Serangoon for the North East Line. NTP+, the mall at New Tech Park, is opposite the station.

Chuan Park is about 600 m from the site. The Orie is about 1.6 km away in Toa Payoh. Thomson Reserve is about 3.4 km away, so it is a different neighbourhood. I include it because it is the most recent big launch in the area.

Distances to other projects are straight lines from the middle of the site. Walking routes are longer. Source for NTP+: EdgeProp.

What the developers paid for the land

With a government land sale, I start from what the developer paid for the land, because that sets the floor for the launch price.

One Chuan Grove sits on two sites that URA sold separately in 2025. The first drew 7 bidders and went for $1,376 per square foot of floor area (psf ppr). The second drew 5 bidders and went for $1,331. The same two developers won both, for $1.33 billion in total.

The Orie is the closest match on land. Its site cost $1,360 in November 2023, about the same as Chuan Grove, and its homes sold at $2,730 psf in the month it launched, 14 months later.

Land has not become cheaper since. The newest site in the area is at Sin Ming Avenue, next to Bright Hill MRT. It drew 7 bidders in September 2026 and went for $1,612, 17% above the first Chuan Grove site 14 months earlier.

URA also has another Chuan Grove site on its Reserve List, large enough for about 930 homes. A Reserve List site only goes to tender when a developer commits to a minimum price, so there is no date for it. If it is sold, it becomes the next launch beside One Chuan Grove.

My take

Before any price list comes out, these are the numbers I would keep in mind.

  • $2.61M is what buyers paid for a new 3-bedroom at Chuan Park, also at Lorong Chuan, mostly two years ago.
  • $2.13M to $2.69M is what a resale 3-bedroom costs today in the 99-year condos around it.
  • $1,376 is what the developers paid for the first site, about the same as The Orie’s land. The newest site nearby has since gone for $1,612.

I also noticed that new and resale prices already overlap here. A resale 3-bedroom at The Woodleigh Residences sold at $2.69M, about the same as a new one at The Orie at $2.68M. So in this area a new home does not always cost more than a resale one.

My own working estimate is that One Chuan Grove launches at an average of about $2,750 psf. The developer has not released prices, so I will update this once the price list is out. For comparison, buyers paid a median of $2,599 psf at Chuan Park, $2,721 at The Orie and $2,863 at Thomson Reserve.

When the prices come out, I would put One Chuan Grove’s 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom prices against these, bedroom for bedroom, before looking at anything else. How I worked this out

  • Prices are from URA caveats, up to November 2026. November is not a full month yet.
  • For the new launches I used every new sale since launch. For resale I used the past 12 months.
  • URA does not record bedrooms, so I matched each sale’s floor area to the project’s unit types from its floor plans and listings. A sale I could not match is left out of the bedroom columns.
  • A box says “Too few sales” where fewer than three sales matched.
  • The neighbours’ figures are past sales. The launch estimate is my own working figure and is not the developer’s price.
