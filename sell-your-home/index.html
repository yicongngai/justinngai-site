The work behind the listing

From preparation to completion.

01

Set a defensible asking price

Compare recent transactions with your home’s size, floor, facing, condition and competition. Asking prices alone do not show what buyers will pay.

02

Prepare the home and its story

Agree on repairs, decluttering, photographs and video that help buyers understand the home. The presentation should show its strengths accurately.

03

Bring suitable buyers through

Plan the listing channels, viewing schedule and follow-up. Review buyer feedback and competing properties rather than leaving the same advertisement running unchanged.

04

Negotiate and plan the handover

Compare offers, financing conditions and completion dates. Coordinate with your lawyer and your next purchase so the sale fits your wider plan.

Before you list

Know what you can use from the sale.

The selling price is not your usable cash. We’ll separate the outstanding loan, CPF refund and sale costs, then work out when the remaining funds are available.

If you’re considering a sale, let’s chat about your home and when you hope to move.
