Developer & site

What is Impressions at Dairy Farm?

Impressions at Dairy Farm is the name ERA’s launch guide uses for the condo planned on the Dairy Farm Walk land sale site in District 23. URA awarded the site on 28 January 2026 to ABR Holdings, LWH Holdings, Macly Capital and RP Ventures. EdgeProp reports that Roxy-Pacific Holdings leads the group and is the project manager.

The site is 29,444.2 sqm, about 316,900 sq ft, on a 99-year lease, with up to 41,222 sqm of floor area. That is a plot ratio of 1.4, so this will be a low-rise project. ERA’s guide expects buildings of up to six storeys, and EdgeProp notes the land slopes.

Two home counts are in print. URA’s estimate for the site is about 480, and ERA’s guide says about 500. The developers have not released the unit mix, floor plans, sizes or a showflat date.

Sources: URA tender award, 28 January 2026 · EdgeProp, 22 January 2026 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is Impressions at Dairy Farm, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Dairy Farm Walk. The Botany at Dairy Farm is about 130 m away and Narra Residences about 270 m. Hillview MRT (DT3) on the Downtown Line is about 770 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit, and Cashew MRT (DT2) is about 910 m. Plan on a walk of ten minutes or more, or a bus.

Dairy Farm Mall is about 400 m away and HillV2 about 1.05 km. Dairy Farm Nature Park is about 600 m to the south, measured to the point OneMap gives for the park.

CHIJ Our Lady Queen of Peace is about 410 m from the middle of the site and Bukit Panjang Primary School about 720 m. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.
