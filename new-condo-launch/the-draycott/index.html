The building

What is The Draycott?

The Draycott is a freehold development on Draycott Park in District 10, completed in 1980. It was developed by Tan Chwee Boon and designed by the late architect Timothy Seow. The site is over 161,000 sq ft, at the corner of Draycott Park and Stevens Road.

There is one 34-storey tower made up of three round stacks, and four-storey blocks with two-bedroom homes and maisonettes. EdgeProp and ERA’s portal both give 132 homes.

Why are homes at The Draycott for sale now?

After the building was completed, homes in two of the tower’s three stacks were sold to individual buyers. The Tan family kept the third stack and leased it out for decades. In late 2024 the family started releasing homes from that stack for sale, and EdgeProp reported in April 2026 that about 15 were still available. Floor plans

The Draycott floor plans and unit sizes

Location

Where is The Draycott, and how near is the MRT?

The Draycott sits between Stevens Road and the top of Orchard Road. Newton MRT (NS21, DT11) is the nearest station, about 700 m away. Orchard MRT (NS22, TE14) is about 880 m away and Stevens (TE11, DT10) about 1.1 km. The Tanglin Club is about 230 m away and The American Club about 410 m.

The roads around the estate are not served by buses, which keeps them quiet. Plan on walking to the MRT or driving.

Anglo-Chinese School (Primary) on Barker Road is about 840 m away. Singapore Chinese Girls’ Primary School and Anglo-Chinese School (Junior) are just over 1 km away. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.

Questions buyers ask

The Draycott: floor plans, tenure and viewings

Where can I see The Draycott floor plans?

The typical and modified four-bedroom plans are on this page, under Unit mix and layouts. Message me for the two-bedroom, maisonette or penthouse plans.

Is The Draycott freehold?

Yes. It is a freehold development, completed in 1980.

How big are the homes at The Draycott?

Most tower homes are 2,637 sq ft. The low-rise homes run from 1,313 sq ft for a two-bedroom to 2,788 sq ft for a maisonette, and the duplex penthouse is 5,339 sq ft.

Is there a showflat?

No. The Draycott is a completed building, so you view the actual home, by appointment. Message me to arrange one.

How many homes are still for sale?

EdgeProp reported about 15 in the family’s tower stack in April 2026. Availability changes, so ask for the current list before you plan a viewing.
