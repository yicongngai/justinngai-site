Developer & site

What is Dorset Gardens?

Dorset Gardens is the name ERA’s launch guide uses for the condo planned on the Dorset Road land sale site in Farrer Park, District 8. URA awarded the site on 16 October 2025 to United Venture Development (2022) Pte. Ltd. EdgeProp reported the bid as a joint venture of UOL Group, Singapore Land Group and Kheng Leong, split 60:20:20, ahead of eight others.

The site is 10,399 sqm, about 111,900 sq ft, on a 99-year lease, with up to 36,397 sqm of floor area. When it bid, the group said it planned 428 homes in two 27-storey towers. ERA’s guide puts the project at about 425 homes.

The developers have not released the unit mix, floor plans, sizes or a showflat date.

Sources: URA tender award, 16 October 2025 · EdgeProp, 9 October 2025 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is Dorset Gardens, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Dorset Road, a few streets north of Farrer Park MRT. The station (NE8) on the North East Line is about 220 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit. Little India MRT, for the Downtown Line, is about 860 m away.

City Square Mall is about 540 m away and Mustafa Centre about 560 m. KK Women’s and Children’s Hospital is about 840 m away.

Farrer Park Primary School is about 370 m from the middle of the site. St Joseph’s Institution Junior is about 900 m away and Hong Wen School about 930 m. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.
