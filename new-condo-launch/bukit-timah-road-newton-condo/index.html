Developer & site

What is the Bukit Timah Road condo?

The Bukit Timah Road condo does not have a name yet. It is the project planned on the Bukit Timah Road land sale site next to Newton MRT. URA awarded the site on 21 November 2025 to HH Investment Private Limited. EdgeProp reports that HH Investment is the Singapore arm of Taiwan’s Huang Hsiang Construction, and that it beat seven other bidders.

The site is 5,899.2 sqm, about 63,500 sq ft, on a 99-year lease, with up to 28,907 sqm of floor area. That is a plot ratio of 4.9, so this will be a high-rise. URA’s estimate is about 340 homes.

The developer has not released a project name, the unit mix, floor plans, sizes or a showflat date.

Sources: URA tender award, 21 November 2025 · EdgeProp, 11 November 2025 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is the Bukit Timah Road site, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Bukit Timah Road, near Newton Circus. Newton MRT (NS21, DT11) is about 100 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit. The station is on the North South Line and the Downtown Line, and Orchard is one stop away.

Newton Food Centre is about 300 m away. United Square is about 850 m and Novena MRT about 1 km.

Anglo-Chinese School (Junior) is about 590 m from the middle of the site and Anglo-Chinese School (Primary) about 610 m. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.
