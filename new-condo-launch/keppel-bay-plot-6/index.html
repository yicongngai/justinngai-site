Developer & site

What is Keppel Bay Plot 6?

Keppel Bay Plot 6 is a residential site on Keppel Island, the small island that holds Marina at Keppel Bay. Keppel’s 2025 annual report lists it as a proposed 84-unit waterfront condominium on 99-year leasehold land of 43,701 sqm (about 470,400 sq ft). The site is held by Keppel Bay Pte Ltd, which Keppel owns in full.

Keppel’s reports still call it Keppel Bay Plot 6. EdgeProp’s new launch directory lists 84 homes by Keppel Bay Pte Ltd at Keppel Bay Vista under the name The Island Residence. Keppel has not published a launch date, floor plans, unit sizes or a completion date.

Two figures differ between sources. ERA’s portal entry shows 86 homes and freehold tenure. Keppel’s own report says 84 homes and 99-year leasehold, and this page uses Keppel’s figures.

Sources: Keppel annual report 2025, major properties · EdgeProp directory. Checked 12 November 2026.

Location

Where is Keppel Bay Plot 6, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Keppel Island, beside Marina at Keppel Bay. The island joins the mainland by Keppel Bay Bridge, so every trip on foot or by car starts across the bridge. Reflections at Keppel Bay and Corals at Keppel Bay are about 300 m away across the water.

Telok Blangah MRT (CC28) is about 810 m away in a straight line and HarbourFront MRT (NE1, CC29) about 860 m. The walk is longer because of the bridge. VivoCity is about 1 km away. The Circle Line became a full loop on 12 July 2026, when Keppel, Cantonment and Prince Edward Road stations opened, so it now runs from HarbourFront on to Marina Bay.

No primary school is within 1 km. Blangah Rise Primary School is the nearest, about 1.4 km away. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.

Straight-line distances measured on OneMap on 12 November 2026. Circle Line: LTA, 14 May 2026.

Questions buyers ask

Keppel Bay Plot 6: launch date, tenure and units

When is Keppel Bay Plot 6 launching?

Keppel has not announced a date. ERA’s portal shows March 2027 as an indicative month, which is provisional. Message me and I will let you know as soon as it is confirmed.

Is Keppel Bay Plot 6 freehold?

No. Keppel’s annual report lists the land as 99-year leasehold, the same as the other Keppel Bay condos.

How many units will Keppel Bay Plot 6 have?

84, according to Keppel’s 2025 annual report. ERA’s portal shows 86.

Is The Island Residence the same project?

EdgeProp lists The Island Residence as 84 homes by Keppel Bay Pte Ltd at Keppel Bay Vista, which matches Plot 6. Keppel’s own reports still use the name Keppel Bay Plot 6.

Are the floor plans and showflat details out?

Not yet. Keppel has not released floor plans, unit sizes or a showflat date. Check these in the sales documents before committing.
