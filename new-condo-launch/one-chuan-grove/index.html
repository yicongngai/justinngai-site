At a glance

What is confirmed about One Chuan Grove?

One Chuan Grove is an upcoming 99-year leasehold condominium at Chuan Grove in the Lorong Chuan area. Sing Holdings now lists 1,056 apartments, seven shops and a childcare centre, in one 24-storey block and four 27-storey blocks.

The developer’s residential total takes precedence here over the 1,055-home figure in the older ERA snapshot. The preliminary information remains dated; current prices, the detailed unit mix and the completion timetable need confirmation.

Developer & site

Who is developing One Chuan Grove?

One Chuan Grove is being developed by Sing Holdings with Sunway Developments. URA awarded two neighbouring Government Land Sales sites at Chuan Grove to Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd.: the first on 17 July 2025 (about 15,832 sqm) and the second on 10 September 2025 (about 14,514 sqm). The two parcels form one development.

Sing Holdings lists 1,056 apartments in one 24-storey block and four 27-storey blocks, with seven shop units and a childcare centre. The developer gives residential sizes from 474 to 1,561 sqft. The bedroom-by-bedroom unit mix, site plan and floor plans have not been released publicly yet.

Sources: URA award, July 2025 · URA award, September 2025 · Sing Holdings project page

Location

Where is One Chuan Grove, and how near is the MRT?

The site is at Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan in District 19. Lorong Chuan MRT (CC14) on the Circle Line is the nearest station, about 530 m from the middle of the site on ERA’s straight-line estimate. From Lorong Chuan it is one stop to Bishan for the North South Line and one stop to Serangoon for the North East Line.

NTP+, the mall at New Tech Park opposite Lorong Chuan MRT, is a short walk away for everyday shopping and food. Check the actual walking route from your preferred block once the site plan is out.

Source: EdgeProp on NTP+
