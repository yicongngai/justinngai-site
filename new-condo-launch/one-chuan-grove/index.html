At a glance

What is confirmed about One Chuan Grove?

One Chuan Grove is an upcoming 99-year leasehold condominium at Chuan Grove in the Lorong Chuan area. Sing Holdings now lists 1,056 apartments, seven shops and a childcare centre, in one 24-storey block and four 27-storey blocks.

The developer’s residential total takes precedence here over the 1,055-home figure in the older ERA snapshot. Prices and the detailed unit mix are not out yet. Check the completion date in the sales documents.

Developer & site

Who is developing One Chuan Grove?

One Chuan Grove is being developed by Sing Holdings with Sunway Developments. URA awarded two neighbouring Government Land Sales sites at Chuan Grove to Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd.: the first on 17 July 2025 (about 15,832 sqm) and the second on 10 September 2025 (about 14,514 sqm). The two parcels form one development.

Sing Holdings lists 1,056 apartments in one 24-storey block and four 27-storey blocks, with seven shop units and a childcare centre. The developer gives residential sizes from 474 to 1,561 sqft. The bedroom-by-bedroom unit mix, site plan and floor plans have not been released publicly yet.

Sources: URA award, July 2025 · URA award, September 2025 · Sing Holdings project page

Location

Where is One Chuan Grove, and how near is the MRT?

The site is at Chuan Grove, off Lorong Chuan in District 19. Lorong Chuan MRT (CC14) on the Circle Line is the nearest station, about 530 m from the middle of the site on ERA’s straight-line estimate. Sing Holdings puts the station about 250 m away, within a five-minute walk. From Lorong Chuan it is one stop to Bishan for the North South Line and one stop to Serangoon for the North East Line.

NTP+, the mall at New Tech Park opposite Lorong Chuan MRT, is a short walk away for everyday shopping and food. Check the actual walking route from your preferred block once the site plan is out.

Source: EdgeProp on NTP+

Site plan & unit mix

One Chuan Grove site plan, unit mix and brochure

The site plan, the unit mix by bedroom, the floor plans and the sales brochure have not been released. This is what the developer has published so far.

  • The site is two Government Land Sales (GLS) plots joined into one, 30,345.8 sqm or about 326,600 sqft.
  • There will be one 24-storey block and four 27-storey blocks, with 1,056 apartments.
  • The plan includes a few shops, an early childhood development centre and a neighbourhood park of about 10,000 sqm that links to the Mei Hwan landed estate.
  • Homes range from 474 to 1,561 sqft. The number of 2 bedroom, 3 bedroom and larger homes is not out yet.

When will One Chuan Grove launch, and when will it be completed?

In its half-year results on 12 August 2026, Sing Holdings said construction was expected to start in the third quarter of 2026 and that the project is on track for its sales launch in the first quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals. The same filing gives 2030 as the expected year of completion.

Is the One Chuan Grove showflat open?

The developer has not published a showflat location or an opening date. Dates you see on other websites are not confirmed by the developer.
