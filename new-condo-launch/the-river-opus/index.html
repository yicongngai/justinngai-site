Developer & site

What is The River Opus?

The River Opus is the planned redevelopment of Valley Point and Fraser Suites Singapore at 491 River Valley Road, in District 10. Frasers Property and Mitsubishi Estate announced it on 30 September 2026 as a 51:49 joint venture, and describe it as a riverfront project along the Singapore River. The land is 999-year leasehold.

The plan is for around 407 homes, including sky villas, with a new 184-unit Fraser Suites serviced residence and shops and restaurants on the same site. The site measures about 144,656 sq ft.

The developers aim to launch the homes for sale in 2027 and complete the project in 2031. Valley Point and Fraser Suites Singapore are expected to stop operating on 31 March 2027. Floor plans, unit sizes and a showflat date have not been released.

The announcement did not name the project. The River Opus is the name shown for 491 River Valley Road on OneMap and on ERA’s portal.

Sources: EdgeProp report of the developers’ joint announcement and OneMap. Checked 11 November 2026.

Location

Where is The River Opus, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on River Valley Road, a short distance west of Great World. Great World MRT on the Thomson-East Coast Line (TE15) is the nearest station, about 610 m from its closest exit. Tiong Bahru (EW17) on the East West Line is about 760 m away across the water, and Havelock (TE16) about 850 m. Great World mall is about 540 m away.

River Valley Primary School is about 970 m away. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.

Questions buyers ask

The River Opus: launch date, showflat and floor plans

When is The River Opus launching?

The developers have said 2027 and have not given a date. Message me and I will let you know as soon as it is confirmed.

Where is The River Opus showflat?

No showflat or sales gallery has been announced. Valley Point and Fraser Suites Singapore are expected to keep operating until 31 March 2027.

How many units will The River Opus have?

Around 407 homes, including sky villas, according to the developers. ERA’s portal shows 400 as a preliminary figure. The 184-unit serviced residence is separate from the homes for sale.

Is The River Opus freehold?

The land is 999-year leasehold. River Green and Promenade Peak nearby are 99-year leasehold.

Are The River Opus floor plans and prices out?

Not yet. The developers have not released floor plans, unit sizes or a price list. Check these in the sales documents before committing.
