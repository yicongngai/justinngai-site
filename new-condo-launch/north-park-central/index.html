What is North Park Central?

North Park Central is the name ERA’s launch guide uses for the redevelopment of Yishun 10, the cinema and shops building beside Yishun MRT. Frasers Property announced the redevelopment on 2 September 2026: a mixed-use project with about 110 homes above shops at street level.

Frasers is targeting the residential launch for mid-2027 and completion in 2031. Yishun 10 is expected to stop operating on 2 March 2027. Frasers calls it the first residential launch in Yishun Central in more than a decade.

EdgeProp reports a site of 39,125 sq ft on 99-year leasehold land, with a total floor area of 117,376 sq ft. Frasers has not released the unit mix, floor plans or sizes. Its announcement did not name the new project.

Sources: Frasers Property announcement, 2 September 2026 · EdgeProp, 2 September 2026 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is North Park Central, and how near is the MRT?

Yishun 10 is at 51 Yishun Central 1, in the middle of Yishun town centre. Yishun MRT (NS13) on the North South Line is about 90 m from the building to its nearest exit. The bus interchange in Northpoint City is about 200 m away.

Northpoint City is next door. Frasers says residents will reach it through the existing underground pedestrian network. Yishun Pond Park is about 470 m away and Khoo Teck Puat Hospital about 690 m.

Ahmad Ibrahim Primary School is about 530 m away. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.
