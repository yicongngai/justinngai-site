What is Lentor Signature?

Lentor Signature is the name ERA’s launch guide uses for the condo planned on the Lentor Central land sale site in District 26. URA awarded the site on 6 March 2026 to GuocoLand (Singapore), Intrepid Investments and TID Residential. EdgeProp reports the venture as GuocoLand with Hong Leong Holdings and TID.

The site is 15,925.8 sqm, about 171,400 sq ft, on a 99-year lease, with up to 47,778 sqm of floor area. The plan reported so far is about 553 homes in three towers, two of 26 storeys and one of 27. It is GuocoLand’s fifth residential project in Lentor Hills.

The developers have not released the unit mix, floor plans, sizes or a showflat date, and they have not confirmed the project name publicly.

Sources: URA tender award, 6 March 2026 · EdgeProp, 2 July 2026 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is Lentor Signature, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Lentor Central, beside Lentor Modern, the mall and condo linked to the station. Lentor MRT (TE5) on the Thomson-East Coast Line is about 350 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit, and Lentor Modern is about 170 m away. The line runs from Lentor to Orchard and Marina Bay without a change of train.

Anderson Primary School is about 1 km from the middle of the site. That is right at the 1 km mark, so the block you choose can decide which side of the line you are on. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.

Thomson Nature Park is about 1.25 km to the west. Lentor Central Residences, still under construction, is on the next plot.

Timing

When will Lentor Signature launch?

GuocoLand expects to launch the project in the first half of 2027, as EdgeProp reported on 28 August 2026. ERA’s public guide of 6 November 2026 also lists it among the launches expected in the first half of 2027.

No preview date, booking date or completion date has been announced.
