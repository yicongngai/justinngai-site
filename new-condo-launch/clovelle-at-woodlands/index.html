What is Clovelle at Woodlands?

Clovelle at Woodlands is the name ERA’s launch guide uses for the executive condominium (EC) planned on the second Woodlands Drive 17 land sale site, in District 25. HDB’s tender closed on 13 January 2026 and the site went to Sim Lian Land and Sim Lian Development. EdgeProp reports three bids.

The site is about 290,400 sq ft on a 99-year lease, with up to 609,867 sq ft of floor area. Two home counts are in print. HDB’s estimate for the site is about 560, and ERA’s guide says about 570.

It is the larger of two ECs coming up side by side. The other is Wynwood Grand by CDL. ERA notes they are the first EC launches in Woodlands since Northwave in 2016.

An EC comes with eligibility and ownership conditions. ERA’s guide says the ECs expected in the first half of 2027 fall under the previous EC rules, which include a five-year minimum occupation period. Confirm with HDB and the developer which rules apply to this project before you plan around them.

Location

Where is Clovelle at Woodlands, and how near is the MRT?

The site runs along Woodlands Avenue 12 at Woodlands Drive 17. Woodlands South MRT (TE3) on the Thomson-East Coast Line is about 310 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit. Woodlands MRT, the interchange with the North South Line, is one stop away, about 1.4 km in a straight line. Causeway Point is about 1.65 km away.

ERA’s guide notes that Woodlands Health Campus and Woodlands Healing Garden are close to both EC sites.

Innova Primary School is about 800 m from the middle of the site and Woodgrove Primary School about 1.05 km. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.

Clovelle at Woodlands: launch date, developer and units

When is the Clovelle at Woodlands launch date?

ERA expects it in the first half of 2027. Sim Lian has not given a date. Message me and I will let you know as soon as it is confirmed.

Is Clovelle at Woodlands an executive condo?

Yes. It is on an EC site sold by HDB, so buyers must meet the EC eligibility conditions. Check yours with HDB first.

Who is the developer?

Sim Lian Group. HDB’s record names Sim Lian Land and Sim Lian Development.

How many units will there be?

About 560 on HDB’s estimate, or about 570 in ERA’s guide. The final count comes with the sales documents.

How is it different from Wynwood Grand?

They are two separate ECs on neighbouring plots at Woodlands Drive 17. Clovelle at Woodlands is by Sim Lian and is the larger one. Wynwood Grand is by CDL.
