What is Chencharu Central Residences?

Chencharu Central Residences is the upcoming 99-year leasehold mixed-use project at Chencharu Close in Yishun. This page previously used the land-sale name, Chencharu Close GLS. The development combines private homes with shops, a hawker centre and a bus interchange.

HDB awarded the site to Evia MCS Pte. Ltd., Gamuda (Singapore) Pte. Ltd. and H108 Pte. Ltd. on 26 September 2025. Gamuda’s public project page describes up to 875 homes and up to 12,600 sqm of commercial space. These are preliminary project figures, not a final unit allocation or released price list.

Why consider an integrated development here?

The practical attraction is having everyday shopping, food and bus connections within the development, with Khatib MRT nearby. Before paying a premium for that convenience, compare the actual home with a completed alternative around Khatib or Yishun. Check usable space, noise, privacy and the route you would take each day.

A growing neighbourhood does not guarantee investment returns. For an own-stay purchase, the layout and your move-in plan matter as much as the surrounding transformation.

The neighbourhood

Chencharu: a new housing area in Yishun

Chencharu is a new housing area between Khatib and Yishun, planned to add about 10,000 homes by 2040, most of them public housing. Chencharu Close, the site of Chencharu Central Residences, is the first private housing site in the area. You may also see it called the Chencharu condo, Chencharu Residences, Chencharu Central or the Chencharu Close GLS.

The site was tendered as a mixed-use development: about 875 homes above up to 135,627 sqft of commercial space, which includes a bus interchange and a hawker centre. The tender set aside at least 58,125 sqft for the bus interchange and 37,674 sqft for the hawker centre, on a site of 316,997 sqft. Gamuda has said the project is to be completed within 84 months of the September 2025 award.

For drivers, LTA expects the North-South Corridor to open in phases from 2029 (the viaduct section from Admiralty Road West to Lentor Avenue) and from 2031 (the tunnel section to the ECP).

Sources: EdgeProp on Chencharu · EdgeProp on the tender · Gamuda announcement · LTA North-South Corridor. Checked 6 November 2026.

Common buyer questions

Chencharu Central Residences launch FAQs

Is this the Chencharu condo, also called Chencharu Residences?

Yes. Chencharu Central Residences sits on the first private housing site in Chencharu, so searches for the Chencharu condo, Chencharu Residences, Chencharu Central or the Chencharu Close condo all point to this project.

When is the launch or showflat preview?

The exact preview and booking dates remain unconfirmed on this page. Preliminary agency schedules differ, and the public developer page checked on 26 October 2026 does not give a firm date. Confirm the latest timetable before arranging a visit or making plans around it.

Have prices and floor plans been released?

A confirmed price list and individual floor plans have not been verified here. Ask for the latest approved materials, then compare a specific unit’s total price, layout, floor and facing. No starting price is advertised on this page.

How many homes will there be?

HDB’s tender estimate and Gamuda’s public project description refer to about 875 homes. The final approved count and detailed mix may differ; check the current developer sales documents.

Is it near Khatib MRT?

Yes. Gamuda describes the project as five minutes from Khatib MRT. The older ERA portal estimate in the MRT section is a distance estimate, not a verified walking route. Check the final access point and the walk you would actually use.

What is integrated with the residences?

The planned development includes commercial space, a hawker centre and a bus interchange. Final operators, opening dates and access arrangements should be checked when released.

What is the TOP or completion date?

A contractual completion date has not been verified here. The older portal’s 2029 date is not presented as a current commitment. Check the developer timetable and sale documents before planning a move.

Is this the developer’s official website?

No. This is Justin Ngai’s independent property information page. Information is dated and subject to confirmation; an enquiry does not reserve a unit.
