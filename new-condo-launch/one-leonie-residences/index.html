Developer & site

What is One Leonie Residences?

One Leonie Residences is a freehold development by Far East Organization at 1 Leonie Hill in District 9, a short walk from Orchard Road. Far East describes 25 residences: four to six bedroom homes and a duplex penthouse, with views of the Orchard skyline. With 25 homes it is one of the smallest new launches in the area.

Far East is taking registrations of interest for preview information. It has not published a preview date, floor areas, floor plans or a completion date yet.

Source: Far East Organization project page. Checked 7 November 2026.

Location

Where is One Leonie Residences, and how near is the MRT?

Leonie Hill is a quiet residential enclave between Orchard Road and River Valley. Orchard MRT on the Thomson-East Coast Line (TE14) is about 390 m away and the North South Line platforms about 480 m, on ERA’s straight-line estimates. Great World (TE15) and Somerset (NS23) are both within about 750 m.

River Valley Primary School is the nearest primary school, about 730 m away. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.
