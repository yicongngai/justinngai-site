Developer & site

What is the Dover Drive condo?

The Dover Drive condo does not have a name yet. It is the project planned on the Dover Drive land sale site, the first plot in the new Dover-Medway neighbourhood beside one-north. URA awarded the site on 31 March 2026 to CNQC Realty (Prime), Forsea Residence and Jianan Realty Investments (1). EdgeProp reports the venture as Qingjian Realty, Forsea Holdings and Jianan Capital, ahead of five other bidders.

The site is 13,517.2 sqm, about 145,500 sq ft, on a 99-year lease, with up to 56,773 sqm of floor area. It is zoned for homes with shops on the first storey. URA’s estimate is about 625 homes. EdgeProp reports about 32,300 sq ft of commercial space, which includes an early childhood centre.

The developers have not released a project name, the unit mix, floor plans, sizes or a showflat date.

Sources: URA tender award, 31 March 2026 · EdgeProp, 26 March 2026 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is the Dover Drive site, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Dover Drive, beside the Ayer Rajah Expressway. one-north MRT (CC23) on the Circle Line is about 300 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit. Buona Vista MRT, where the Circle Line meets the East West Line, is about 1.05 km away.

Fusionopolis is about 350 m away. Rochester Mall is about 810 m and The Star Vista about 950 m. Dover-Medway is a new neighbourhood, and ERA’s guide notes that for now it has fewer everyday shops and places to eat than a mature town centre.

Fairfield Methodist School (Primary) is about 180 m from the middle of the site. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration. Anglo-Chinese School (Independent) is about 640 m away.
