What is Bedok Rise Residences?

Bedok Rise Residences is the name ERA’s launch guide uses for the condo planned on the Bedok Rise land sale site, beside Tanah Merah MRT in District 16. URA awarded the site on 2 December 2025 to Bellis Residential Pte. Ltd. EdgeProp reported the winning bid as Allgreen Properties’, ahead of nine others.

The site is 20,293.6 sqm, about 218,400 sq ft, on a 99-year lease, with up to 32,470 sqm of floor area. That is a plot ratio of 1.6. URA’s estimate is about 380 homes. EdgeProp describes it as the last land parcel fronting Tanah Merah MRT.

Allgreen has not released the unit mix, floor plans, sizes or a showflat date, and it has not confirmed the project name publicly.

Sources: URA tender award, 2 December 2025 · EdgeProp, 27 November 2025 · ERA’s guide to first-half 2027 launches, 6 November 2026. Checked 14 November 2026.

Location

Where is Bedok Rise Residences, and how near is the MRT?

The site is on Bedok Rise, on the south side of New Upper Changi Road. Tanah Merah MRT (EW4) on the East West Line is about 100 m from the middle of the site to its nearest exit. The Changi Airport branch starts here, and EdgeProp reports that the station is slated to become an interchange with the Thomson-East Coast Line.

Sceneca Square, the shops below Sceneca Residence, is about 200 m away on the other side of the station. Bedok MRT and Bedok Mall are one stop west, about 1.9 km in a straight line, and Expo MRT is about 1.95 km to the east.

Temasek Primary School is about 960 m from the middle of the site and Bedok Green Primary School about 1 km. Both sit close to the 1 km mark. Check the official distance on OneMap before relying on it for registration.
