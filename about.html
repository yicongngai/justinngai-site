For me, a useful first conversation is about your current home and what you want to change. You might need more room, want to be closer to family, or be working out whether an upgrade is affordable. The property search comes after we understand that.

When we look at a new launch, I’ll compare the unit with nearby resale homes and work through the layout and total purchase cost with you. For an HDB upgrade, we also need to know what you can use from the sale and how the dates line up. A purchase can look affordable on paper and still be awkward to fund at the wrong point in the move.

If you’re selling, I start with recent transactions and the homes buyers can choose from today. Then we plan the asking price, photographs and video, viewings and handover around what you want to do next.

Outside work, I enjoy outdoor adventures. I like having something to look forward to away from the desk and the next viewing.

If you’re thinking about a move, let’s chat about where you are now and what you have in mind. It’s fine if you’re still working out the details.
